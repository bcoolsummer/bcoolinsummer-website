BCOOL SUMMER INC — REBUILT WEBSITE

1. Upload the CONTENTS of this folder to the hosting public_html / web root for bcoolinsummer.com.
2. Configure the domain DNS to point to your web host, and turn on HTTPS/SSL.
3. Verify that all pages load on phone and desktop.
4. Confirm actual legal company name, phone, location, offered services, and every policy with the business owner before Stripe review.
5. The contact form is a DESIGN PREVIEW and does not send messages. Phone number is the functional contact option. A real web-form delivery service must be configured before enabling digital submissions.
6. Photos and Google Fonts are externally loaded and require internet access. Replace photos with company-owned images for more credibility.
7. Stripe alone decides account verification or payout availability.

Files are static HTML, CSS and JS and work on most ordinary web hosts.
